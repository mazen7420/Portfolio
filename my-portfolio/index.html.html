Meal Planner

Flutter · Dart · Mobile App

A complete meal-planning experience

Meal Planner helps users discover meals, explore cuisines and categories, save favorite recipes, and organize their week through a built-in calendar.

Flutter Dart Authentication Calendar
View project
Users need an easy, low-friction way to discover meals and organize what they'll actually cook during the week, instead of losing recipe ideas across screenshots and bookmarks.
A mobile app combining meal discovery, search, and favoriting with a calendar view — so planning a week of meals takes minutes, not scattered notes.
A complete meal-planning experience built end-to-end in Flutter, from authentication through daily use.
